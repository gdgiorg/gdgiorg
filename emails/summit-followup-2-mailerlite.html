What to know before you arrive

Hi {$name}, the National Summit opens this week. Here's what to expect, whether you're joining us in Abuja or online.

If you're attending in person

The summit runs both days at the Main Conference Hall, Office of the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Shehu Shagari Complex, Three Arms Zone, Abuja. The venue has step-free access and reserved seating for wheelchair users and persons with visual or hearing disabilities. If you told us about an accessibility requirement when you registered, our team is already preparing for it, if anything's changed, just reply to this email.

If you're joining virtually

We'll send the Zoom and live YouTube links on the morning of each day, 14 and 15 October, so keep an eye on your inbox. Both days will carry sign-language interpretation and live captioning for virtual attendees.

The programme

Two days of policy dialogue tracks, speaker sessions, and the signing of the Abuja Declaration on Disability-Inclusive Climate Action. Full session details are on the summit page.
